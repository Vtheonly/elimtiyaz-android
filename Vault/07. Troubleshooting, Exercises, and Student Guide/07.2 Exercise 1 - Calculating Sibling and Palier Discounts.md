# 07.2 Exercise 1 - Calculating Sibling and Palier Discounts

#exercises #pricing #discounts #algeria #worked-example #math

---

## 1. Problem Scenario and Given Data

The **Benali family** is enrolling 3 children at El-Imtiyaz for the 2026-2027 academic year in Boumerdès:

1. **Child 1 (Amine)**: Enrolling in **3AS (3ème Année Secondaire - Terminale BAC)**.
   - *Status*: Continuing high school student.
2. **Child 2 (Sara)**: Enrolling in **1AM (1ère Année CEM)**.
   - *Status*: Just passed the 5AP exam and entered Middle School (Transition de Palier).
   - *Academic Standing*: Ranked #1 across the primary cycle (awarded the Academic Excellence prize).
3. **Child 3 (Yanis)**: Enrolling in **2AP (2ème Année Primaire)**.
   - *Status*: Continuing primary student.

**Payment Option Chosen**: The father pays the entire family's annual tuition in full via bank transfer before **June 30**.

---

## 2. Applicable Tariffs and Rules from Policy

- **Base Tuition Schedule**:
  - `3as`: $450,000\text{ DZD}$ ($45,000,000\text{ centimes}$)
  - `1am`: $330,000\text{ DZD}$ ($33,000,000\text{ centimes}$)
  - `2ap`: $265,000\text{ DZD}$ ($26,500,000\text{ centimes}$)
- **Discounts Reference**:
  - **Palier Transition (`passage_palier`)**: Fixed $-10,000\text{ DZD}$ ($-1,000,000\text{ centimes}$) for Sara.
  - **Sibling Scale (`sibling_fixed`)**: 3 enrolled children $\implies$ Child 1 gets $0\text{ DZD}$, Child 2 gets $-5,000\text{ DZD}$, Child 3 gets $-5,000\text{ DZD}$.
  - **Academic Excellence (`highest_average`)**: $-10\%$ percentage discount for Sara.
  - **Full Annual Prepayment (`full_annual`)**: $-10\%$ percentage discount across all 3 children.

---

## 3. Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation

### 3.1 Child 1: Amine (3AS)
1. **Gross Tuition ($T_1$)**: $450,000\text{ DZD}$.
2. **Fixed Discounts**: None (Eldest sibling $\implies 0\text{ DZD}$ sibling deduction, no palier crossing).
   $$\text{Subtotal}_1 = 450,000\text{ DZD}$$
3. **Percentage Discounts**:
   - Full Annual ($-10\%$): $P_{\text{rate}} = 10\%$.
   $$\text{Discount Amount} = 450,000 \times 0.10 = 45,000\text{ DZD}$$
4. **Net Tuition for Amine**:
   $$T_{\text{net}, 1} = 450,000 - 45,000 = \mathbf{405,000\text{ DZD}}\quad (40,500,000\text{ centimes})$$

---

### 3.2 Child 2: Sara (1AM)
1. **Gross Tuition ($T_2$)**: $330,000\text{ DZD}$.
2. **Fixed Discounts**:
   - Palier Transition (5AP $\rightarrow$ 1AM): $-10,000\text{ DZD}$
   - Sibling Deduction (2nd child): $-5,000\text{ DZD}$
   - *Total Fixed*: $-15,000\text{ DZD}$.
   $$\text{Subtotal}_2 = 330,000 - 15,000 = 315,000\text{ DZD}$$
3. **Percentage Discounts**:
   - Academic Excellence: $-10\%$
   - Full Annual Prepayment: $-10\%$
   - *Cumulative Rate*: $10\% + 10\% = 20\%$.
   $$\text{Discount Amount} = 315,000 \times 0.20 = 63,000\text{ DZD}$$
4. **Net Tuition for Sara**:
   $$T_{\text{net}, 2} = 315,000 - 63,000 = \mathbf{252,000\text{ DZD}}\quad (25,200,000\text{ centimes})$$

---

### 3.3 Child 3: Yanis (2AP)
1. **Gross Tuition ($T_3$)**: $265,000\text{ DZD}$.
2. **Fixed Discounts**:
   - Sibling Deduction (3rd child): $-5,000\text{ DZD}$
   $$\text{Subtotal}_3 = 265,000 - 5,000 = 260,000\text{ DZD}$$
3. **Percentage Discounts**:
   - Full Annual Prepayment: $-10\%$.
   $$\text{Discount Amount} = 260,000 \times 0.10 = 26,000\text{ DZD}$$
4. **Net Tuition for Yanis**:
   $$T_{\text{net}, 3} = 260,000 - 26,000 = \mathbf{234,000\text{ DZD}}\quad (23,400,000\text{ centimes})$$

---

## 4. Total Family Settlement

$$\text{Total Family Invoice} = 405,000 + 252,000 + 234,000 = \mathbf{891,000\text{ DZD}}\quad (89,100,000\text{ centimes})$$

*Total Family Savings across all discounts*: $(450,000 + 330,000 + 265,000) - 891,000 = 1,045,000 - 891,000 = \mathbf{154,000\text{ DZD}}$ saved!

---

## 5. Tips, Tricks, and Student Traps

> [!WARNING]
> **Trap 1: Applying Percentage Discounts Before Fixed Deductions.**
> Naively calculating $20\%$ off the gross $330,000\text{ DZD}$ gives $66,000\text{ DZD}$ discount, which inflates the deduction. School policy mandates: **Fixed deductions must be subtracted FIRST to establish the adjusted subtotal.**

> [!CAUTION]
> **Trap 2: Double-Counting the Eldest Sibling.**
> The sibling discount applies to the 2nd child onwards ($N - 1$). Giving the sibling discount to the 1st child is an accounting error.
